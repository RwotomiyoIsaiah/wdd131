Nile Haven River Resort

Website Planning & Architecture Document

1. Site Name & Branding

Site Name: Nile Haven River Resort

Optional Domain Availability: nilehavenresort.com (or nilehaven-ug.com)

Rationale: The name combines "Nile" to immediately anchor the location to the iconic river and "Haven" to evoke tranquility, luxury, and retreat. It clearly communicates to international and local travelers that the venue is a peaceful getaway along the Nile.

2. Site Purpose

The Nile Haven River Resort website acts as a digital gateway and primary booking engine for guests seeking eco-luxury accommodation and adventure along the Nile River. The site provides comprehensive details on cottage bookings, river adventures (such as white-water rafting, sunset boat cruises, and kayaking), dining menus featuring local cuisine, and direct reservation tools to convert site visitors into confirmed guests.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

The following key user scenarios drive the content structure and user experience of the site:

  • Scenario 1 (Adventure Traveler): "What adventure activities (like white-water rafting or river cruises) are offered by the resort, and can I book them together with my accommodation?"
  • Scenario 2 (Weekend Getaway Guest): "What are the nightly rates for riverfront luxury cottages, what amenities are included, and how do I check availability for this weekend?"

5. Typography

Two complementary Google Web Fonts were selected to balance rustic elegance with digital readability:

  • Primary Heading Font: Cinzel (Serif)

    Used for top-level headers (h1, h2, h3). Its classical serif proportions lend an upscale, elegant feel suited for a premium river resort.

  • Body Font: Lato (Sans-Serif)

    Used for all paragraph text, navigation links, and lists. Highly legible on mobile screens and desktop monitors across all font sizes.
