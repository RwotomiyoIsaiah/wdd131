Nile River Resort

Website Planning & Architecture

Site Name & Logo

Site Name: Nile River Resort

Domain: nileresort.com (or nileresort-ug.com)

Rationale: The name contains "Nile" to reference the iconic river and "Resort" to evoke luxury and retreat. Thus communicating to clients that the venue is a peaceful getaway along the Nile.

Site Purpose

The Nile River Resort website is a gateway and primary booking service for guests seeking accommodation and adventure along the Nile River. It contains comprehensive details on bookings, river adventures like white-water rafting, sunset boat cruises among others and menus featuring local dishes

Scenarios ?

Popular scenarios you might be Curious about

  • Scenario 1 (Adventurer): "What adventure activities (like white-water rafting or river cruises) are offered by the resort, and can I book them together with my accommodation?"
  • Scenario 2 (Weekend Guest): "What are the nightly rates for riverfront luxury cottages, what services are included, and are they available throught the year?"

Typography

Two Google Web Fonts were selected to balance elegance with readability:

  • Primary Heading Font: Cinzel (Serif)

    Used for headers. Provides an elegant feel perfect for a river resort.

  • Body Font: Lato (Sans-Serif)

    Used for all paragraph text, navigation links, and lists.
